Learning in context

Consider the journey, not just the session.

Think about your group size, preparation, practice time, and follow-up support when comparing learning options.

A focused workshop can introduce a skill and create time to practise. A connected journey adds opportunities to try it at work, return with questions, and build confidence through feedback.

Choose the approach around the change you want to see. Consider how managers will support learners, which resources people can use afterward, and when you will review progress together.

Plan beyond the delivery day.

Allow time for preparation, workplace application, and a follow-up conversation so the learning has a clear place in your team's routine.
