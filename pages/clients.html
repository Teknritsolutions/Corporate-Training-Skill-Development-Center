Learning in context

Make progress a shared conversation.

Agree what you want to notice, make room for reflection, and connect learning to the work your team is doing.

Start with a shared picture of success: the conversations, decisions, or daily habits your team wants to improve. This gives learners and managers a practical reference point throughout the journey.

After each session, make time to discuss what people tried, what changed, and where they need support. Combine learner reflections with workplace examples to decide the next useful step.

Keep the conversation going. A brief manager check-in can connect a workshop insight to a real task, customer interaction, or team decision.
